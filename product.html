Specifications

Specifications

MWD Ball-Screw Drive | NDR006-I

  • Robust 3 mm pitch ball-screw design with plug-and-play installation for easy integration.
  • Field-proven reliability delivering extended service life in demanding downhole environments.
  • Captured shaft architecture helps reduce downhole mechanical failures.
  • Qualified for operation up to 180 °C, suitable for high-temperature drilling environments.
  • Improved top-bearing design increases bearing life and reduces failure rates.
  • High-strength Chromium-Molybdenum steel construction for superior durability.
  • Dynamic load rating: 3900 N | Static load rating: 7200 N.
  • 3/16” OD input shaft size for greater shear strength.
  • Precision linear alignment with <0.002" runout for smooth operation and reduced wear.
  • Hardened stainless-steel shafts for improved strength and corrosion resistance.
  • Enhanced spline nut design for improved load handling and reliability.
  • Low-pitch high-load version available with 3900 N dynamic / 7200 N static load rating.
  • Significantly reduced operating costs through improved durability and reliability.
  • Carbide coating on integrated driver shaft for improved shaft and seal wear.

MWD Ball-Screw Drive | NDR006-I

Product Benefits

The assembly is optimized for repeatable performance, controlled stroke, and durable operation in high-temperature drilling conditions. EETP engineers and tests each drive in Calgary to ensure consistent quality before shipment.

The MWD Ball-Screw Drive is a high-load linear actuator designed to support reliable mud-pulse telemetry in demanding drilling environments. By converting rotary motor input into precise linear motion, the drive helps reduce vibration-induced wear, lower service time, and minimize unplanned tool trips.
